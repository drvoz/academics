What this shows & how to read it

This is the schedule for the plan — when each piece of work happens, and what has to finish before the next piece can start. Each bar is one task; its length is how many months that task takes. Tasks are grouped into three phases, top to bottom.

Pick a start month and year at the top. Because many tasks depend on earlier ones, changing the start moves everything that follows — that is the "cascade." The colors match the map: Strategy & governance, Operations, Enabling. The striped bar with the gold outline is the priority item — redesigning clinician-researcher contracts.

Hover a bar to see its dates and what it waits on. Click a bar to make that task longer or add a delay, and watch everything downstream shift. Durations are planning estimates for discussion, not commitments.
