Greatest Care and Love requires access to the best care and treatments. Academics and research are therefore extensions of the clinical mission, not adjacent activities.

Research is successful when it meaningfully changes what OSF can do for patients.

Become a regional destination for complex care and a nationally recognized research leader — expanding local access to cutting-edge therapies, establishing OSF as a referral destination, and attracting world-class talent.

Implementation Roadmap
Phase 1 · < 6 months

Create visibility, accountability & readiness

  • VP of Research & proactive ROC with defined decision rights
  • Defined strategy, priority service lines & growth objectives
  • Research GL & P&L framework for spending visibility
  • Compliant effort reporting & streamlined IRB deployed
  • Core systems selected & deployed (Cayuse, CTMS, eReg)
Phase 2 · 6–12 months

Build the capabilities required for growth

  • Framework to evaluate, prioritize & manage partnerships
  • Readiness criteria to qualify non-SFMC spoke sites
  • Standardized operating model with KPI tracking & intake
  • Centralized contracting, budgeting & post-award admin
  • Physician-investigator talent model with protected time
Phase 3 · 12 months +

Scale research across the ministry

  • Consolidated research operations across all institutes
  • QA & monitoring function for enterprise quality
  • Expanded CRC & research support workforce
  • Research active across qualified spoke sites
  • Analytics, lab/Rx capacity & AI supporting execution
Measures of success: Best Care (measurable outcomes) · Best People (physician-scientist recruitment & retention) · Best Plan (diversified, sustainable portfolio).  Board goal: sustainable $140M competitive extramural portfolio by FY10 with nationally recognized programs in cancer, cardiovascular, neuroscience & pediatrics.
